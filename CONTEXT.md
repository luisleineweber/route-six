# Pokémon Playthrough Planner

The planner helps players choose a team and check which Pokémon they can obtain
at a selected point in a game.

## Language

**Milestone**:
A point in game progress, such as a story event or the point before a Gym battle.
_Avoid_: Badge count as the name for all progress.

**Badge**:
A reward from a Gym battle. Badge count describes only part of game progress.

**Team**:
The set of up to six Pokémon that a player chooses for a playthrough.

**Level limit**:
The recommended maximum Pokémon level at a milestone. It determines which
level evolutions the planner considers available when the limit applies.
_Avoid_: Level cap.

**Game trade**:
A Pokémon exchange with a character in the selected game.
_Avoid_: Trade without a named kind.

**Player trade**:
A Pokémon exchange with another player.

**Choice conflict**:
A team includes Pokémon whose required choices cannot all be made in one
playthrough.

**Availability**:
Whether a player could obtain a Pokémon at a milestone under the selected
rules. It does not mean that the player already owns that Pokémon.

**Acquisition path**:
A source and the steps needed to obtain a Pokémon from it.

**Source**:
A way to obtain a Pokémon, such as a wild encounter, gift, fossil revival,
game trade, or breeding.

**Capability**:
A game action that the player can use after meeting its requirements, such
as Surf or a fishing method.

**Breeding**:
The production of an egg from two compatible Pokémon at the Day Care.

**Parent pair**:
Two distinct Pokémon used together to produce an egg. A compatible pair meets
the selected game's species, gender, and Egg Group rules.

**Egg Group**:
A group that determines which Pokémon can breed together under the game's rules.
