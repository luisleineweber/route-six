# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pokémon Emerald players who plan a team while they play. They use a computer or
a phone next to their game. They need to check what they can obtain at each point.

## Product Purpose

Plan up to six Pokémon. Explain direct sources, evolution, breeding, level
limits, and choice conflicts. Use the same access rules in both planners.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, helpful. Use a Pokémon field guide style, as confirmed by the user.
Keep game names and facts easy to find. Use short, direct text.

## Anti-references

Do not add a world map, move database, accounts, or a data import service.
Do not let game-style decoration hide controls or status text.

## Design Principles

- Show the source and required action with each access result.
- Keep the team when the player changes progress.
- Separate individual access from team choice conflicts.
- Keep settings shared between the two planners.
- Use local, checked data and make data errors visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use visible labels, keyboard controls, and visible focus. Pair status colours
with text. Support narrow screens and reduced motion. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA.
